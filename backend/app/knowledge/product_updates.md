# Approved MentorMe chatbot product update

Approved by the repository owner in the local chatbot request on 2026-09-03.
This supplements the public-page sources for the chatbot only; it does not change website pages.

## Course journey and recognition

- Courses use a guided journey of up to 90 levels, combining learning, practice,
  assessments, projects, milestone achievements, portfolio proof and career preparation.
- Learners can earn milestone/achievement certificates at meaningful skill checkpoints
  during the journey. Preserve the published full Pro entitlement of nine milestone certificates.
- Successful completion of the full course journey earns a MentorMe Course Completion
  Certificate. This is distinct from milestone recognition.
- Do not invent an overall certificate total, external accreditation, additional award
  requirements or a complete level-by-level syllabus.

## Career opportunities and support

- Completing the full course journey and required milestones makes learners eligible
  for MentorMe-supported internship opportunities with startups.
- Availability and selection depend on each opportunity and its requirements.
  Never guarantee selection, placement, salary or employment.
- Mentor sessions can unlock at meaningful stages during the journey; they are not
  necessarily available from the first level.
- Live interview-preparation support can unlock at later stages toward career readiness.
- Exact unlocks and availability depend on the selected course and progress. Direct
  visitors to their personal journey after Sign Up or Sign In; this public chatbot
  cannot inspect account status or confirm a learner's individual eligibility.
- No exact mentor/interview unlock level or new plan-specific entitlement was supplied.
  The new staged-unlock guidance takes precedence over a blanket chatbot statement
  that mentor support is unavailable. Preserve published prices, six-month Pro access,
  Free entitlements, and Career Accelerator's coming-soon status.

## Conversation requirements

- Keep all eight existing starting choices and add Certificates.
- Link certificates to milestones, course completion and internship eligibility;
  link projects/portfolio to achievements and career readiness; link career readiness
  to mentor sessions, interview preparation and internship opportunities.
- Request follow-up and natural contact requests begin optional collection.
  Reuse a known name; otherwise ask for an optional name, then email, then mobile.
- Preserve format validation, Skip, Cancel, temporary session storage and the clear
  statement that contact details have not been sent and no callback is booked.
- Capitalise simple wholly lowercase names conservatively, preserving mixed case.
